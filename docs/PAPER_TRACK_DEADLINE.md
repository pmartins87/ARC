Snapshot: 2026-08-25

Two organizer-controlled pages currently disagree by one day:

- **Kaggle Paper Track timeline:** final submission deadline **2026-11-09 23:59 UTC**;
- **ARC Prize 2026 overview page:** says **November 8, 2026 — Papers due**.

The Kaggle Paper Track page is the actual submission host and explicitly says all deadlines are 11:59 PM UTC unless otherwise noted. It also says only submitted Writeups before the competition deadline are considered.

## Project scheduling rule

Use:
- **external/operational deadline:** 2026-11-09 23:59 UTC, per the Kaggle submission host;
- **internal safety deadline:** 2026-11-08 23:59 UTC.

This prevents us from relying on the extra day while the organizer pages remain inconsistent. Re-check both pages during M7/M8 and obey any later official update.

## Eligibility reminder

The Kaggle Paper Track states that a valid final entry must include:
- a submitted Kaggle Writeup;
- Media Gallery;
- attached Public Notebook;
- optional Project Link.

The paper must document a solution for ARC-AGI-2 or ARC-AGI-3, and the evaluation asks for the corresponding leaderboard submission ID. High leaderboard score is a rubric component but is not a stated minimum eligibility threshold.

Therefore the engineering project must preserve a reproducible competition submission ID even if the Paper Prize becomes the stronger prize path.
